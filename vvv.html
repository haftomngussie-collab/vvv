About Haftom and His Mission

With a PhD in Honeybee Health, Dr. Haftom Gebremedhn combines deep scientific knowledge with more than 18 years of practical experience in apiculture. He promotes 100% pure and natural honey sourced from Tigray.

He has trained local beekeepers, contributed to 23+ research publications (ORCID), and received international certifications from Ghent University and Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

His mission is to deliver pure honey while supporting rural beekeeping communities.

About Tigray’s Honey

Discover the unique taste of Tigray’s honey, harvested from northern Ethiopia’s diverse flora. Varieties include:

  • Water White
  • Extra White
  • White
  • Extra Light Amber
  • Light Amber
  • Amber

Renowned for purity, nutrition, and natural healing properties.
